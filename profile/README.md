# Bleu

We build products, modernize systems and put AI to work — from Florianópolis,
Brazil, for companies in Brazil, the US, and Europe. [bleu.builders](https://bleu.builders)

Most of our engineering is public. A sample of what we've shipped and maintain:

**For CoW Protocol** — 16+ initiatives over 30+ months around the core protocol:
[cow-py](https://github.com/bleu/cow-py) (Python SDK, built by Bleu and upstreamed
to CoW Protocol), [scaffold-composable-cow](https://github.com/bleu/scaffold-composable-cow)
(125+ forks), programmatic order tooling, governance dashboards.

**For Silo Finance** — built the entire data layer, end to end: subgraphs,
multichain APIs, liquidation infrastructure. Infrastructure costs cut 80%.

**For Perk** — the whole product team for 3+ years: 16 apps in production,
including a global M&M's launch that reached 1.5M people at 99.984% uptime.

**In Brazil** — open financial infrastructure like
[stellar-brl-corridor](https://github.com/bleu/stellar-brl-corridor),
a BRL/PIX corridor on Stellar with audited Soroban primitives.
